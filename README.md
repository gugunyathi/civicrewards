## The CivicRewards & Public Service Delivery Awards## Celebrating the Champions Keeping Johannesburg Alive.

We don't just reward daily reports—we celebrate outstanding impact. CivicRewards hosts annual and quarterly awards to honor the citizens, suppliers, and municipal regions making the biggest difference in the City of Johannesburg.

These high-profile events and their cash/asset prizes are fully open for corporate sponsorship.

## Award Categories Available for Sponsorship:

- The Active Citizen Award: Recognizing outstanding community members who log, track, and mobilize neighbors to protect local infrastructure.

- The Outstanding Service Provider Award: Honoring the municipal contractor or supplier with the fastest, highest-quality repair turnaround times.

- The Premier Ward & Region Awards: Celebrating the cleanest, most responsive, and most civically engaged wards and regions (Regions A–G) in Johannesburg.

## Sponsorship Benefits for the Awards Night:

- Headline branding on all event marketing, live streams, and trophies.

- Presentation rights for the primary category prizes (e.g., "The [Your Brand] Active Citizen of the Year").

- Direct networking access to municipal leaders, top-tier contractors, and community influencers.
